About Us

Learn more about Arawaza and our commitment to excellence in martial arts equipment.

Our Story

Arawaza is a globally recognized martial arts brand with a genuine passion for innovation, quality, and performance. We design and manufacture high-performance martial arts equipment that supports athletes during training and competition.

Founded on strong martial arts traditions, Arawaza has continuously pushed the boundaries of design and technology for over 25 years. Our products are trusted by professionals around the world.

We believe in discipline, excellence, and continuous improvement. These values guide every product we create.

The Meaning of Arawaza

The word "Arawaza" originates from Japanese martial arts culture and represents a powerful, advanced, and highly disciplined technique.

Similar to martial arts masters who achieve excellence through dedication and years of practice, Arawaza products are designed to help athletes reach their highest potential.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide world-class martial arts equipment that combines safety, comfort, durability, and innovation.

We are committed to supporting martial artists worldwide by delivering products that inspire confidence and performance.

  • Integrity
